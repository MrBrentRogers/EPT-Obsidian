# Double Tubeway Station

Physical Characteristics

- The tubeway station is dark, except for the glowing red light in the floor of the damaged tubeway.
- The air is stale but breathable.
- The floor is littered with fallen rubble and miscellaneous bits and pieces. Many footprints of different types, human and otherwise crisscross all over. No time of passing can be determined.
- There are two tubeways. One is blocked some 10 m down with a wreck of some sort. Various flotsam and jetsam have been cast down upon it. The other is open and the forcefield that would have prevented accidentally falling in has long ago ceased to function. A matching opening is present in the ceiling over each hole.
    - Pressing the RED will call a car.
    - YELLOW lights up to indicate that it is on its way.
    - BLUE lights when the car has arrived.
- CONTROL ROOM – console that is severely damaged. Cabinets are empty
- CORRIDOR – Sliding doors are stuck. Resist with STR 16
    - Corridor is severely damaged. Completely blocked at the end.
- CAVERN – a rough cavern has been excavated. It is hidden by fallen wall and ceiling.
    - Search -2 to discover it.
    - Cavern is low and cramped with rough floor. It generally slopes upward from the station.
- STAIRS – ancient stairs, much worn and slick with moisture and slime. Decorated with worn bas relief. Difficult to determine the era. Ceiling is of various cuts and types of stone.
- CORRIDOR – similar to the stairs.
    - WILL -2 to smell cinnamon
- ROOM 1
    - Double doors of stone. Open into the corridor.
    - Very high ceiling off into the shadows, wherein hide the Vorodlá.
    - Frescos and bas reliefs, mostly damaged. Maybe of followers of Ksarul or Gruganu.
    - Vorodlá (number determined by party capability)
    - PHYS: 12 DEFT: 11 INTL: 12
    - WILL: 12 PSYC: 2 Size Mod: 0
    - Initiative: 2/4/6 Movement: 3 ground, 14 flying
    - **Defenses:** Melee: 3 Missile: 3 Magic: 6
    - Armor: 2/0 Hits: 20 Energy: 10
    - Attacks Hit: Dmg: Other:
    - Bastard Sword 14- 5/7/9 poisoned (see below)
    - **Regeneration:** 1 per round, can’t regenerate fire damage.
    - **Total Pain Resistance:** only defeated (and slain) when reduced to negative their starting Hits.
    - **Camouflage:** +1 Defense, Stealth, etc. in darkness or dim light. **Heal From Death:** the Vorodla’s healing abilities continue to work even after it has been ‘killed’.
    - **Poison:** Attribute Drain, PHYS check at +4 or lose 1 point in a random attribute (PHYS, DEFT, INTL, or WILL) until healed. Non- magical healing of this effect is difficult, suffering a -4 penalty on its chances of success.
- ROOM 2
    - Apartments of the Jagji.
    - If the battle of with the Vorodla takes more than 10 rounds, the Jagji will come to investigate.
