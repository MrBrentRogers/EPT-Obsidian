[[Runequest Tekumel.pdf]]
Hairy six-legged creatures which build underground dens covered with a thin layer of sod. From these they leap upon passing prey. They have slender rod-like antenna and a barbed prehensile tongue with which they carry their victims down into their lair to feed at their leisure. Large groups are usually immune from the Dnelu's depredations -- it prefers solitary prey.
