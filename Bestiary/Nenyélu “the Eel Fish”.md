They also have a pair of rudimentary hands just behind their snake-like heads, which they use to hurl or strike with a jagged stone. They possess only rudimentary intelligence, however. Nenyélu range up to 4.5 meters in length and about 15 centimeters in diameter. Their bite is poisonous, and even if healed, a victim usually sustains some neural damage.

## D&D5e  
### Nenyélu  
Medium beast, unaligned  
Armor Class 14 (natural armor)  
Hit Points 27 (5d8 + 5)  
Speed swim 40 ft.

STR 6 (-2) DEC 21 (+5) CON 13 (+1) INT 2 (-4) WIS 12 (+1) CHR 3 (-4)

Skills Perception +3  
Senses darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13  
Languages -  
Challenge 2 (450 XP)

ACTIONS  
Multiattack. The Nenyélu makes two attacks each round, bit, punch or rudimentary weapon (rocks).  
Bite. +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target, 9 (1d6+5) piercing damage, and the target must make a DC 11 Constitution saving throw, taking 7 (2d6) poison damage on a failed save, half on a successful one. Victims who fail the saving throw are also Poisoned (as condition), and can remake the save at the end of each of their turns. It hit again by the poison and fail the save, they become paralyzed. A successful save ends both conditions.  
Rock. Melee or Ranged attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., ranged (20/60 ft.), one target, Hit: 7 (1d4+5) bludgeoning damage.  
Punch. Melee hit: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target, hit: 1 bludgeoning damage.
