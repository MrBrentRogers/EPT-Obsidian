[[Tekumel Bestiary.pdf]], p.26

Riverbanks, lakeshores, and coastal flats.

This heavily armored, lumbering, dark green-gray, quadruped has three powerful sets of heavy jaws hidden in protected cavities beneath its carapace. It extrudes these separately, one above the other, out to a distance of about 3 meters. It is also armed with a spiked tail that it uses to defend its rear. The Ghár grows to about 3.7 to 4.6 meters long and has a turtlelike shell covered with spines, ridges and excrescences. The inside of this shell is iridescent, like mother-of-pearl; it is worth 80 to 1,600 Káitars.

## D&D5e
### Ghár 
Huge monstrosity, chaotic evil

AC: 19 (natural armour)  
HP: 105 (10d12 +40)  
Speed: 10 ft., swim 40 ft.

STR 21 (+5) DEC 14 (+2) CON 19 (+4) INT 4 (-3) WIS 15 (+2) CHR 5 (-3)

Saves. Str +9, Con +8  
Skills: Perception +2, stealth +6  
Senses: passive Perception 12  
Languages: None  
Challenge 10 (5900 XP)

Hold Breath. The Ghár can hold its breath in water for 1 hour.  
Water Predator. When the Ghár ends its turn submerged in water and not adjacent to any hostile creatures, it may make a stealth check to become hidden. If the water is over 5 feet deep, they make this check with advantage.

Multiattack. The Ghár can make two melee attacks: one bite and one tail slash.  
Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 18 (2d12 + 5) piercing damage.  
Tail Slash. Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 16 (2d10 + 5) bludgeoning damage, and target must make a DC 17 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.
