Low mountains and dense forest.  
This ropy, blue-black, feathered, snake-like creature attacks viciously and fearlessly. Its teeth contain poison which produces a hideous rotting gangrene, and the victim of a Vringálu must apply Tsúral-buds or use sorcerous healing quickly. Vringálu poison is also dangerous to the touch. The wings of these creatures are used by tanners to make excellent leather for armor, boots, etc. A tanner will pay 100 Káitars per wing.
