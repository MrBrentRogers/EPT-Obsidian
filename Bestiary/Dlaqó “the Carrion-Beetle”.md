Almost anywhere in the Underworlds, ruins, caverns, etc.

This great armoured beetle-like creature has an iridescent green carapace, six legs, three amber-coloured eyes, and powerful scissors-like mandibles . It lives on carrion and usually does not attack unless provoked. Dlaqó range from fist-sized up through twenty-footlong monsters . This is another escapee from some ancient zoo.

Great armored creatures with iridescent green carapaces, six legs, and powerful scissors-like mandibles. They attack only if they can surprise a group, or if the party is a small one (1-12). Otherwise they scuttle away. They are carrion-feeders, and may be found in any area containing a recently-slain being. They range in size from 2m to 6m long.

Challenge [small] 1/4 (50 XP), [medium] 2 (450 XP), [large] 4 (1,100 XP)  
Hard Shell. The Dlaqó has such a hard exterior shell that it has 3 DR (ignores damage from every hit.) This reduction is improved to 6 DR if medium size and 9 DR when large. The only weakness is its soft underbelly, where it does not benefit from any DR. A Dlaqó can be flipped over with an Athletics (Str) check DC 11/13/15. While flipped the Dlaqó has Speed 0, no reactions, and disadvantage to attack. The Dlaqó will right itself on its next turn, taking all its movement.

---

Hit: 6 (2d6 - 1) slashing damage. Increasing to 10 (2d8 + 1) at medium and to 14 (2d10 + 3) at large.  
Notes:  
The Dlaqó is a skittish beast and will scuttle away when confronted by large numbers of foes – twelve or more. In regions they inhabit they can be encountered wherever freshly slain creatures are found (33% chance0. Usually found in packs, 2-12 creatures. 20% small, 40% medium, 40% large. (*doubled in lairs).
