Swamps around Penóm and Púrdimal.  
This blue-purple swamp plant has puffy, veined fronds that exude deadly contact poison. It moves very slowly and can attack a sleeping person within a foot or so of the ground. The victim takes 4 damage every minute (10 rounds), PHYS check at -2. It grows to 1.2 to 1.8 meters in diameter and resembles a great cabbage.
