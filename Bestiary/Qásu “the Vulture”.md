[[Bethorm - The Plane Of Tekumel.pdf]]
Almost all swamps and bogs.  

This is the largest and most vicious of Tékumel's many scavenger birds, growing .6 to .9 meters in height and with a wingspan of 3 to 5.5 meters. It is purple and black and is hence sacred to Lord Hrü'ü. It subsists on carrion, small animals, etc. but will attack a human being if annoyed. It is not edible.
