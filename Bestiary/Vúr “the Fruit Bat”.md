Deep forest and coastal jungle. 

This is a large, nocturnal, sooty-black, bat-like flying creature, which grows to .9 or 1.2 meters in length and has a much larger wingspan. It has three eyes, like the Shánu'u, to which it is related. It lives off fruit, vegetables, and small rodents but its bite can be painful - and possibly dangerous because of infection.
