Locations: Deep ocean and coastal waters . Stories ofAkho inhabiting  
Lake Parunal and the swamps around the city of Purdimal  
are untrue.

These sinewy, dull grey monsters range from small specimens about 10 feet in length, to the commoner size of 15-30 feet, to the largest individuals, which may be 50-75 feet long! Two years ago a dead Akh6 was found washed up on a beach in Livyanu that must have measured nearly 100 feet long and 30 feet in diametre! The Akh6 has twenty steel-hard tentacles (the largest are the thickness of ships' hawsers!) with which it seizes its victims . The creature has one glaring red eye set atop a cucumber-shaped body, beneath which lies its huge maw, filledwith leafy membranousmaterial which eats away a victim with an acid secretion . An Akh6 usually attacks the hull of a ship, and larger specimens can crush it to splinters . The beast may also try to snatch defenders from the ship's decks with its tentacles . These monsters swallow almost everything they grab, and treasures havebeen foundin the remains of dead Akho, consumed who knows how long ago.

Amphibious. The Akhó can breathe air and water.  
Legendary Resistance (3/Day). If the Akhó fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.  
Actions  
Multiattack. The Akhó makes four attacks: one bite and three with tentacles (no more than two tentacle attacks on a given target.) It can do its Swallow attack in place of the bite attack.  
Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +17 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target  
Hit: 20 (2d10 + 9) piercing damage and 21 (6d6) acid damage.  
Tentacle. Melee Weapon Attack: +17 to hit, reach 25 ft., one target  
Hit: 22 (2d12 + 9) bludgeoning damage. The target is grappled (escape DC 25) and the target is restrained until this grapple ends, the Akhó can grapple up to six targets this way.  
Swallow. The Akhó makes one bite attack against a target it is grappling. If the attack hits, the target is also swallowed, and the grapple ends. While swallowed, the target is blinded and restrained, it has total cover against attacks and other effects outside the Akhó, and it takes 21 (6d6) acid damage at the start of each of the Akhó's turns. An Akhó can have only one creature swallowed at a time.  
If the Akhó takes 40 damage or more on a single turn from the swallowed creature, the Akhó must succeed on a DC 27 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or regurgitate the creature, which falls prone in a space within 10 feet of the Akhó. If the Akhó dies, a swallowed creature is no longer restrained by it and can escape from the corpse by using 15 feet of movement, exiting prone.  
Legendary Actions  
The Akhó can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The Akhó regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.  
Detect. The Akhó makes a Wisdom (Perception) check.  
Tentacle Attack. The Akhó makes a Tentacle attack.  
Smash and Grab (Costs 3 Actions). The Akhó charges with a smash and tries to grapple multiple targets. The Akhó moves up to 60 feet and ends its movement with a smash attack, one target, +17 to hit, on success does 31 (4d10 + 9) bludgeoning damage, double damage to inanimate targets. Each creature within 25 feet of the Akhó must succeed on a Grapple (escape DC 25) check or take 22 (2d12+ 7) bludgeoning damage and be grappled and restrained until the grapple ends. The Akhó makes an attempt on each target in range until each target has been attempted once or until the Akhó has six grappled targets (whichever comes first.)

Note. The Akhó will often attack the ship itself rather than the occupants. When it does so, it does double damage to the ship.
