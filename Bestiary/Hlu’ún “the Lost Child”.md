The Tsechélnu Flats and Layóda Swamps of Tsolyánu, the Flats of Gyógma of Háida Pakála, the Sharúna Lowlands of Salarvyá, and the south of Shényu.  
This gauzy-winged, humanlooking insect is about the size of a twelve-year-old boy. It has four very long limbs, a round head with huge eyes, no visible mouth or nose, and a glistening, slimy coating that is very sticky. It sits in the midst of swamps or on a hummock in a pool, looking very much like a mummy or a child clothed in gauze. When its prey approaches, it flies up, lands upon the victim, and wraps it in sticky folds. When the victim has been captured in this way, the Hlu'ún extrudes a proboscis from below its head and feeds upon the juices of the prey. It is particularly feared around the city of Penóm.
