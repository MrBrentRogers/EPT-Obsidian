All deserts except the Dry Bay of Ssu'úm. 

A round, bald-looking, blackish, ugly lump of a creature that buries itself near water in the sand. It grows to about ¾ meters in diameter and is harmless unless some animal steps upon its hiding place. When this happens, it thrusts a bony, barbed, spear-like extension violently up through the soil and into the victim from beneath, usually killing it. It then sucks the animal's bodily fluids down into its hiding place. A nomad can recognize the peculiarly glassy look of the sand above an Utánakh and avoid it. This creature is inedible and harmless if dug out of its burrow.
