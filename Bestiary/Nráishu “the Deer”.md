A six-legged, deer-like herbivore, black or dark brown, with a long-snouted face. Its large eyes see well in the dark, and it is semi-nocturnal. Its front paws are armed with claws for digging roots. If attacked it will flee, though it fights when cornered or wounded. A family herd consists of six to ten members. Its meat is edible but almost too sweet.
