Palmetto forests of the Tsechélnu Flats, the swamps of Penóm and Púrdimal, and along streams, lakeshores, etc.  

This needle-thin, brown-green insect looks like a pointed twig 25 to 75 centimeters long. It has six legs that resemble thin sticks and add to its camouflage. Its flexible, round mouth has projecting spines that move in circular sequence, giving the effect of a hollow rotary drill-bit. With these it can bore a hole the size of a fist into its prey. Larger specimens are said to be able to drill a hole right through a Chlén-hide breastplate.
