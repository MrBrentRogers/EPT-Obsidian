Almost any dry or desert region. Other varieties are found in the swamps around Penóm, in the jungles of M’mórcha, etc.  
This creature is a "segmented" colony creature. It consists of up to fifty little separate beings, each with six tiny legs, a mouth, etc., which join themselves together in a snake-like "train". Most are black, although the "Mighty Epéng" of the Penóm region is a dull maroon in hue. The sting of the front "mouth" of this creature is very painful though not always fatal. The bite of the "Mighty Epéng", however, is instantly lethal.
