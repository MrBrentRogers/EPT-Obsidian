## Káika “the Duck”
[[Tekumel Bestiary.pdf]], p.53
![[kaika (duck) - the tekumel bestiary p53.webp]]

[[Bethorm - The Plane Of Tekumel.pdf]]
Domesticated. Found everywhere.  
This white or grey duck-like, egg-laying bird grows as big as a turkey. It is domesticated in the Five Empires but is also found wild. It is descended from a mutated Terran species.
## Sahulén “the Golden Bird”
[[Bethorm - The Plane Of Tekumel.pdf]]
Deep forest, especially northern Livyánu.  
Alternately pronounced “Sahelún”. This shy, golden-yellow game bird is edible. Its plumage is used by the Livyáni for costumes.
