[[Tekumel Bestiary.pdf]], p.27

The shallow seas around the Tsolei Archipelago, the waters off the coast of Haida Pakala, and the Tutaita Shallows of Yan K6r. It is rare elsewhere.

This tan-coloured, vaguely humanoid creature is so named because of its resemblance, both in appearance and in odour, to a corpse which has been several days in the water. It employs its pupilless, white eyes to hypnotise and paralyse its victims (as many as six at once!) . Sailors say that looking into a Ts6ggu's eyes is to lose one's soul, and only sorcerous healing cures the paralysis . The Ts6ggu does NOT paralyse itself, if shown itself in a mirror.
