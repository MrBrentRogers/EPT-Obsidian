# Hura Crepitans

_Sandbox Tree/dynamite tree_  
Hura crepitans, the sandbox tree, also known as possumwood and jabillo, is an evergreen tree of the spurge family, native to tropical regions, and rain forests.

They grow to 60 metres (200 ft), and its large ovate leaves grow to 2 feet (0.61 m) wide.

The sandbox tree's fruiting bodies are large capsules which can explode when ripe, splitting into segments and launching seeds at 70 metres per second (250 km/160 mph) and as far as 100 metres (330 ft).

---
## D&D5e

Disturbing the tree activates the fruit, causes 1d6 to explode, causing 1d6 piercing damage each to everything in a 90 ft radius. Dexterity save DC 15 for half damage.
