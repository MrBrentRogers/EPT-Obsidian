Woods, forests, copses, and clear terrain.  

This small, fast, black or brown fox-like mammal bites but is not very dangerous. It is hunted and eaten. It burrows to make a lair for its young. It has litters of 6-7 "pups," like a dog, although it is not related to the Terran animal.
