Bronze automatons set to guard dimensional nexus points by the ancients, as well as watch over other treasures. There are many varieties of Ru'un, since they could be manufactured in any form. Essentially, they are war robots.
Most Ru'un can see invisible planes and hear sounds inaudible even to a Pe Choi. They obey only the Incomparable Eye of Command and the Amulet of Ruling the Ru'un (and even these are not infallible). One common type is covered with afine network of wires imparting a powerful electric shock to anyone touching it. This type also carries an enchanted sword atthe end of one arm, and a steel-bolt thrower in the other.

## D&D5e

### Ru’ún

Ru’ún are a variety of robots and droids created for some purpose in the distant past. They may have humanoid features but they do not look at all human, unlike the Yéleth who were designed to look and act human. From small probes to massive haulers, Ru’ún can be found virtually anywhere. They were used for communications, personal accompaniment, maintenance, or combat.   
Ru’un Names  
While most have a name, the name is usually given to them by their creator, so it reflects the creator's race. They also have a string of four letters and numbers, a manufacturing serial number or code.  
Names: M-271, M-3891, M-SK1, F-12KJ, F-402, F-31928  
Ru’ún’s are unique as the kind of Ru’ún that was needed in the distant past forms their physical abilities. Translation and communications, guard, or perhaps a repair Ru’ún.  
Living Construct. Your creature type is construct instead of humanoid. You do not need to eat, drink, or sleep. You only breathe for purposes of heat dissipation. Instead of sleeping, you enter an inactive state for 4 hours each day. You do not dream in this state; you are fully aware of your surroundings and notice approaching enemies and other events as normal.  
Construct Healing. Ru’ún can heal by resting after combat, as normal, but cannot heal from severe damage (missing limbs) without parts from other construct.  
Fragile Circuitry. The electronics that suffuse your form can easily be damaged by electrical discharges. You have vulnerability to lightning damage.  
Ru’ún Traits  
All of the features lined out below are assigned a point value. Pick a number of features equaling a total of no more than 5 points.  
Ability Score Increase.  
[1 - 2] Ability Score Increase. Maximum of +2 to any ability.  
Age. With proper maintenance, robots are theoretically immortal, and do not die of old age.  
Alignment. Robots have an inclination towards law over order, but favor neutrality over good or evil. That being said, a robot’s neural net is complex enough to exhibit any alignment that humanoids are capable of.  
Size. Robots are sized appropriate to their purpose. You can choose whether you were built as a Medium robot or a Small robot. If you choose Small, your Dexterity score increases by 1, and you reduce all movement speeds below by 5 feet. You weigh between 150 and 300 lbs.  
Speed. Your base speed is 30 feet. Choose one form of locomotion.  
[1] Forced air: You take in air through a vent and force it out beneath you, allowing you to hover a 6 inches off the ground. You ignore difficult terrain.  
[0] Legs (pair): You are bipedal and can walk similar to a normal humanoid.  
[1] Legs (multiple): You have three or more mechanical legs that allow you to climb surfaces similar to an insect. You have a climb speed equal to your walking speed.  
[.5] Wheels: You have three or more wheels that allow you to move around quickly. You add 5 feet to your base walking speed, but navigating obstacles such as stairs counts as difficult terrain. When you take the Dash action, you can increase your speed by an additional 10 feet as long as you move in a straight line.  
Languages. You can speak, read, and write any three languages of your choice.  
Manipulators. You were built with up to two manipulators that serve as your hands. Both of your manipulators are the same model. Choose one of the following manipulator models:  
[0] Hand: This is a multi-digit manipulator with three to five digits. Some hands are designed to mimic humanoid hands. You can do anything with a hand manipulator that a humanoid can do with their hands.  
[.5] Pincers: A pincer is a two-fingered, claw-like appendage that focuses all the grip strength on a pair of opposed surfaces, rather like a pair of tongs. Pincers allow the robot to manipulate and lift objects without much difficulty, but objects specifically made for humanoid hands (like firearms) are usually beyond the pincers’ ability to operate. Unarmed strikes with your manipulators deal 1d6 + Strength modifier bludgeoning damage, or 1d4 if Small size.  
Ru’ún Specializations  
[.5] Brave. You have advantage on saving throws against being frightened.  
[+1 OR -1] Damage Resistance OR Vulnerability. You gain Resistance or Vulnerability to either Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing, Fire, or Acid damage.  
[.5] Darkvision. You have superior vision in dark and dim conditions. You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can’t discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.  
[.5] Extra Language. You can speak, read, and write 2 extra languages of your choice.  
[.5 /5ft.] Extra Movement. Your base walking speed increases by 5 feet each time this feature is taken.  
[1.5] External Casing. +1 to natural armour class  
[1] Camouflaged Stealth. You can attempt to hide even when you are only lightly obscured or only take the hide action in a round without moving.  
[.5] Natural Weapon. You are proficient with your unarmed strikes, which deal 1d4 bludgeoning damage on a hit.  
[1] Relentless Endurance. When you are reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you can drop to 1 hit point instead. You can’t use this feature again until you finish a long rest.  
[.5] Skills or Tool Proficiency. You gain proficiency in an additional skill or tools of your choice.  
[.5] Vehicle proficiency. You gain proficiency with a vehicle of your choice (water, land, flying)  
[1.5] Toughness. Your hit point maximum increases by 1, and it increases by 1 every time you gain a level.  
[0.5] Amphibious. You have a swim speed equal to your walking speed.  
[1] Powerful Build. You count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift.   
[1] Weaponized Arms. Your arms are limitedly morphable. As an action, you can morph one arm into a melee weapon you are proficient with, as long as the weapon is not heavy or two handed. You can use a bonus action to morph your arm back into normal form.
