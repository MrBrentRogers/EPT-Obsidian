[[10. The Underworlds  - The Tsuru'úm]]

---
[[Tekumel Bestiary.pdf]], p.31

The upper levels of the Underworlds, caverns, ruins, and desolate wastelands (and mountains).

These scuttling, greyish-brown, six-limbed furry beasts range up to some four feet in length and three feet tall. They are bipedal and have vestigial tails, sharp, horny beaks, and three eyes (hence they probably originate on one of the Hlaka worlds). They live on refuse and carrion and will attack only when they outnumber their opponents . Their terrible stench warns a party of their presence at a distance of 50 feet-even through doors!

These scuttling, grayish-brown, six-limbed furry beasts range up to 1.2 meters in length and .9 meters tall. They are bipedal and have vestigial tails, sharp, horny beaks, and three eyes (hence they probably originate on one of the Hláka worlds). They are attracted to carrion. If the party enter a room containing a dead body, there is a 33% chance that Kúrgha have been attracted. They will attack only when they outnumber their opponents. Their terrible stench warns a party of their presence at a distance of 16 meters – even through doors!
