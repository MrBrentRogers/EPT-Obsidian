Woods, untraveled rural areas throughout Tsolyánu, Mu'ugalavyá, northern Livyánu, and most of the far Northeast. Not known in Salarvyá.  

A flat, disc-shaped fungus that grows to various sizes and looks like a scattering of yellow and white coins upon the ground; if touched, it itches furiously and may kill someone who touches it and is allergic to it.

---
## D&D5e
Upon contact make a DC 10 Constitution save. Failure causes severe irritation and one level of Exhaustion.
