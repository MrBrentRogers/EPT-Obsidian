## D&D5e
### Banshee / Hurú’u  
Medium undead, chaotic evil

Armor Class 12  
Hit Points 58 (13d8)  
Speed 40 ft., (hover)

STR 1 (-5) DEC 16 (+3) CON 12 (+1) INT 12 (+1) WIS 14 (+2) CHR 5 (-3)

Saving Throws WIS +2, CHA +5  
Damage Resistances Acid, Lightning, Thunder; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from silvered  
Damage Immunities Cold, Necrotic, Poison  
Condition Immunities Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained  
Senses Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10  
Languages Common  
Challenge 4 (1,100 XP)

Detect Life. The banshee can sense the presence of creatures up to 30 feet away that aren’t undead or constructs. They know the general direction they’re in but not their exact locations.  
Howling Presence. The Hurú’u howls making anyone within 10 ft. have the deafened condition. Spellcasters cannot cast spells requiring verbal components within this area of effect. The howling can be heard 200 ft. away.  
Undead Nature. A Hurú’u doesn't require air, food, drink, or sleep.  
Actions  
Corrupting Touch. Melee Spell Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 12 (3d6 + 2) necrotic damage.

Wail (Recharge 5-6). The Hurú’u releases a mournful wail, provided that it isn’t in sunlight. This wail has no effect on constructs and undead. All other creatures within 30 feet of it that can hear her (not including those deafened by Howling Presence) must make a DC 13 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, a creature drops to 0 hit points. On a success, a creature takes 10 (3d6) psychic damage.
