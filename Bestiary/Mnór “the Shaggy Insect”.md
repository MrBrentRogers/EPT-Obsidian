Semi-desert and grassland.  
These creatures are semiintelligent and can use very simple tools. They are ovoid in form, with twenty long, slender legs, and are covered with silky pearlgrey fur (which makes them valuable as throwrugs). A Mnór will normally try to flee, but if attacking or attacked, it fights to the death, using rocks or a heavy club. Its huge, faceted eyes are its most vulnerable feature, and if a blow shatters an eye, the Mnór will turn and flee. These creatures gather all sorts of glittering objects in their lairs.
