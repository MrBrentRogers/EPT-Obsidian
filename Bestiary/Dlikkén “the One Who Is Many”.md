This segmented, worm-like creature has segments that may break off into separate individuals; each has its own set of eight legs, and if cut off at the jointure, a segment will run off into the underbrush to grow a whole new creature. Only the front segment has a mouth, however, and it cannot divide itself voluntarily. It is blackish-blue in color, nocturnal, and grows to a length of 2.6 meters in deep forest. The peasants of Sa'á Allaqí say that it is sacred to Lord Ksárul, and devotees of that deity do not kill it unless attacked.

> THE DLIKKÉN THAT FLING  
> The Dlikkén is a creature of Tékumel that breaks into motile segments when struck with sharp weapons. This is a new arboreal variant that uses its hind end to fling its last segment at the enemy.  
> The small units latch on painfully, grappling such that the foe can’t use two arm limbs, cast spells, or kick the grappler.
