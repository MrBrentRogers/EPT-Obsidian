This creature consists of a large grayish white gas bag and a huge, single, bulging eye. Káyi hang high in the air in dark corners and drop their thin, fibrous tentacle-nets down upon prey passing below. These nets are covered with sharp, hollow spines which suck the juices out of a victim. If cut free immediately, the Káyi does not have time to sink these hair-like spines into its prey, and hence does minimal damage. Káyi can be brought down by flaming arrows, but they usually remain out of reach of swords and other hand weapons, though sometimes a spear, pike, or halberd will reach them.

## D&D5e
Káyi “the Eye” 
(modified Death Kiss (VGtM 124)), 
Large aberration

Number appearing: 80%: 1d6; 20%: 3d10

Armor Class 13 (natural armor)  
Hit Points 31 (5d10+4)  
Speed 0, fly 40 ft.

STR 12 (+1) DEC 16 (+3) CON 12 (+1) INT 4 (-3) WIS 14 (+2) CHR 10 (+0)

Saving Throws Dex +6, Wis +5  
Skills Perception +5, Stealth +6  
Senses darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 15, blindsight 20 ft  
Damage Vulnerability: Fire  
Languages NA  
Challenge 110 (5900 XP)  
Fiery Explosion. A creature within 5 feet takes 14 (4d6) fire damage from the Káyi exploding when it’s hit points drop below 0.

ACTIONS  
Multiattack. Three Tentacle attacks and/or Blood drain.  
Tentacle. Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 15) if it is Huge or smaller creature. Until this grapple ends, the target is restrained, and the káyi can’t use the same tentacle on another target. The káyi has ten tentacles.  
Blood Drain. One creature grappled by a tentacle of the káyi must make a DC 16 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the target takes 11 (2d10) necrotic damage, and the káyi regains half as many hit points.
