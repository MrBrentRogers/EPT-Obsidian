Deep forest and fields throughout the Five Empires, but especially in northeastern Tsolyánu and Sa'á Allaqí.  

This small, vicious, carnivore has six legs, a beak-like snout filled with teeth, sleek black fur and a bushy tail. If attacked or cornered, it fights ferociously to the death. Hunters fear it for this reason since it lives in much the same environment as the Jakkóhl and looks superficially like it.
