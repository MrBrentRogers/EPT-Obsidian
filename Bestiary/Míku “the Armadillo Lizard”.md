The Dry Bay of Ssu’úm, the Desert of Eyági, and the barrens around Kayál Peak in eastern Tsolyánu and southeastern Sa'á Allaqí.

Adults of this carnivorous species stand about 1.2 meters high. They are a dull, sandy gray in color. They have a short, stumpy tail and six limbs, the back pair of which is used for locomotion and the front two pairs as hands. The Míku is protected by banded armor, like the ancient Terran armadillo, but its short, snouted head resembles certain lizards. This species lives in families of five or six individuals and subsists on small game, bird eggs, and insects. It will attack small parties of humans who come too close to its semi-subterranean lair, however. The natives of Kayál Peak say that the meat of this animal is excellent when barbecued.
