Location: Deserts. Milumanayá, Chürstállu, and the Dry Bay of Ssu'úm, bt not farther east.

The Anátl is a snad-skate: a triangular creature 3-6 feet long, with a hard upper carapace and many short legs below. It is said to be related to the Ngóro; q.v. The Anátl does not really fly but glides for distances of up to 60-90 feet between dunes, using the aid of the wind. It has three small eyes hidden under the frontal rim of its carapace. Its mouth is at the bottom of its body, and when it attacks, it falls upon a victim and chews downward, clamoing itself to the target with short, powerful retractable legs. It eats almost any living thing, animal or vegetable, and is usually seen in flocks of 20-50. The nomads usually fall prone and hide beneath their desert-claks when Anátl are sighted. The carapace is used as a bowl or carrying-vessel by the tribesmen but has no other value.
