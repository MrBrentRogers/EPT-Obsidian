Forest and swamps.  
Blue-green nocturnal reptiles with long beaks set with three rows of needle teeth. Their six eyes see easily in the dark, and they usually hunt during dark overcast or even stormy nights, when their prey are blind, or nearly so, thus giving the Giriku a significant advantage in combat. They emit a peculiar repellent musty odor.
