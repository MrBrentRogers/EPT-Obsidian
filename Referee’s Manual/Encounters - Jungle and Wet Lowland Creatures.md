
## Jungle and Wet Lowland Creatures

Wet lowlands consist of (1) low-lying plains: occasional bogs, fens, and marshes; (2) swamps: largely water with interspersed islets and thickets; and (3) dense coastal swamps, sometimes called "mangrove swamps," often home to the horrid "Food of the Ssú."
