This artifact is usable by any Mihálli. Non-Mihálli magic users or priests of Sorcery level 14 and up can also operate it. All others take 3/5/7 mental damage if they attempt to use it. This Mihálli artifact has many functions, but only one can be used per Action:

- Energy Bolt: 3/5/7 energy damage, 12” range
- Force Wall: 3/3 protection in a 1” diameter around the user. Only works on non-magical attacks.
- Invisibility: +1 displacement defense bonus
- Gas Cloud: 3” diameter adjacent to user, does 2/3/4 damage to all human and nonhuman species except the Shén and the Ssú. Ignores armor, but victims can hold their breaths.

*This weapon comes in various strengths, represented by its  
color: the weakest one is light purple, then bright silver, then  
bright gold, then lambent, translucent blue.  
Nexus Travel: Mihálli have a natural WILL/ -1 skill to locate and  
open inter-dimensional nexus points. Skill checks can also be  
made to determine where an open nexus point leads. All uses  
of this skill require a full turn. Difficulty penalties apply when  
seeking nexus points to planes that are “distant”. Fumbles  
when seeking a portal lead the Mihálli to a random nexus  
point. There is a bonus of +2 to open a located nexus point.

hazards immediately emerge.

## Ball of Immediate Eventuation

This artifact is usable by any Mihálli. Non-Mihálli magic users or priests of Sorcery Circle 14 and up can also operate it. All others take 10 psychic damage if they attempt to use it. This device comes in various strengths, represented by its color:

**Light Purple**: DC12; +4 Spell Attack; Casting as 3rd level spells.  
**Bright Silver**: DC13; +5; Spell Attack; Casting as 4th level spell slot.  
**Bright Gold**: DC15; +7 Spell Attack; Casting as 5th level spells.  
**Lambent [light of fire]**: DC15; +7 Spell Attack; Casting as 6th level spells.  
**Translucent Blue**: DC17; +9 Spell Attack; Casting as 7th level spells.

This Mihálli artifact has many functions, but only one can be used per Action. The device has 15 spell levels that recharge at dawn.

(1)Fog Cloud; (2)Invisibility; (3)Stinking Cloud; (3)Lighting Bolt; (5)Bigby’s Hand; (5)Cloudkill; (5)Wall of Force.
