Deserts range from the powdery white sands of the Desert of Sighs in Milumanayá, to the gravelly wastelands of the Great Desert of Galai in northwestern Mu’ugalavyá, to the Desert of Eyági’s gritty, glass-like sand, to the parched dunes of the Dry Bay of Ssu’úm in southern Ssá Allaqí, to the rocky barrens of the Sleeping Desert in Mihállu, to the vast, wind-swept emptiness of the Plains of Glass in the far east of the continent. All of these have their own life-forms, only some of which can be included here.
